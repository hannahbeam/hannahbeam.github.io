Academic Focus

I am a Broadcast and Digital Journalism major at Syracuse University in the S.I. Newhouse School, where I have fully embraced the energy and excitement of telling stories that matter. My interest in media began in middle school, and that spark has grown into a true commitment to becoming the kind of communicator who can inform, engage, and energize audiences. Whether I am writing scripts, producing audio, or hosting on air segments, I love the challenge of translating complex information into something clear, accessible, and actually interesting.

Reporting Experience

As a reporter and anchor for NCC News and WAER, I have covered everything from community events to public policy to major campus initiatives. I have learned how to write for multiple platforms, speak live without sounding robotic, and work under tight deadlines while still maintaining accuracy and intention.

Leadership and Campus Roles

I am highly active across campus. As Vice President of Ottos Army, I help run one of the largest student organizations at Syracuse, connecting thousands of students to athletics through messaging, game day promotions, and creative campaigns. As a Newhouse Peer Advisor, I mentor first year students and help them adjust to the fast pace of college life and media programs.

Career Direction

In the long term, I hope to build a career that blends media, storytelling, and strategic communication. I love journalism, but I am also energized by the broader world of media, including brand storytelling, sports communication, audience engagement, and creative content production. My goal is to take my experience in on air reporting, writing, leadership, and community building and apply it to roles that require creativity, strategy, and strong people skills.

Personal Approach

At my core, I am a communicator who values curiosity, collaboration, and initiative. Whether I am anchoring a live segment, planning a themed event, or helping a nervous freshman plan their schedule, I bring energy, humor, and an authentic desire to help people feel informed and included.
